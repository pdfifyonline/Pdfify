Security and Privacy

How PDFify keeps your documents private: files are processed in your browser and never uploaded.

Your files stay with you

When you use a PDFify tool, your file is read by your own browser. The conversion happens on your device. We do not receive, store or see your documents.

Encrypted connection

The website is served over HTTPS, so the pages and scripts you receive cannot be changed on the way.

Passwords

When you use Unlock PDF, the password is used only inside your browser to open the file. It is not sent anywhere.

Accounts

Accounts are handled by Supabase, a trusted authentication provider. Your password is stored only as a secure hash, never as plain text. API secrets are stored only as one-way hashes.

Report a problem

If you find a security issue, please email pdfify963@gmail.com.
