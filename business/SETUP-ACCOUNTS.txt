HOW TO TURN ON ACCOUNTS (about 10 minutes, free)
=================================================
The login, sign up, forgot password and account pages are already built. They only need a free Supabase project.

STEP 1  Create the project
  - Go to https://supabase.com and sign in. Click "New project". Choose any name and a strong database password.

STEP 2  Create the table for API keys
  - In Supabase open "SQL Editor" > "New query".
  - Open the file supabase-setup.sql from this folder, copy ALL of it, paste it, click "Run".

STEP 3  Tell Supabase your website address
  - Authentication > URL Configuration:
      Site URL:        https://pdfify.online/account/
      Redirect URLs:   https://pdfify.online/account/
                       https://pdfify.online/reset-password/
  - Authentication > Providers > Email: keep "Confirm email" ON (recommended).

STEP 4  Copy your two values
  - Project Settings > API:
      Project URL            (looks like https://abcdxyz.supabase.co)
      anon public key        (a long text)
  - Open config.js in this folder and paste them between the quotes. Save.
  - NEVER paste the "service_role" key anywhere.

STEP 5  Upload
  - Upload config.js (and any changed files) to the root of your website. Wait a minute.
  - Open https://pdfify.online/signup/ and create a test account. Open the email link, then log in.

OPTIONAL: Google and Facebook buttons
  - Authentication > Providers: switch on Google and/or Facebook and follow Supabase's short guide
    (you must create an app on Google Cloud / Facebook Developers and paste its keys into Supabase).
  - Until you do this, those two buttons show an error from Supabase. If you do not want them, tell me and I will remove them.

EMAIL SENDING
  - Supabase's built-in email is only for testing and is limited to a few emails per hour.
    Before many people sign up, add your own SMTP (for example Brevo or Resend) in Authentication > SMTP Settings.
